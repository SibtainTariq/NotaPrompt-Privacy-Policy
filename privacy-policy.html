NotaPrompt Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the NotaPrompt Android app (package name com.sibtain.notaprompt). Thank you for using NotaPrompt. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights when using our application.

1. Information We Collect

NotaPrompt collects only limited technical information that is needed to operate the application, including:

  • Device information, such as model and OS version
  • IP address (our service provider receives it like any internet request)
  • Crash reports and diagnostic data
  • Application performance data
  • Anonymous usage counts: when you open or copy a prompt, the app updates a counter so we can see which prompts are popular. These counts are not linked to your identity.

If you use the Favorites feature, the prompts you save are stored only on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers. We do not require users to create an account to browse prompts.

NotaPrompt does not show advertising and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide prompt content and images
  • Show which prompts are popular
  • Improve application performance
  • Fix bugs
  • Maintain security
  • Improve user experience

3. Third-Party Services

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may only be shared when required by law, or with our service provider (Google Firebase) to operate the application, as described above.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable security measures, including encrypted (HTTPS) transmission, to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

7. Your Choices

You may:

  • Uninstall the app to stop all future data collection by NotaPrompt
  • Remove saved Favorites at any time inside the app
  • Request information about, or deletion of, any data associated with your use of the app by contacting us at the email below

We will respond to and process such requests within a reasonable time.

8. Children's Privacy

NotaPrompt is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.
